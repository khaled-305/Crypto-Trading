# Manual spot setups — versioned definitions

Approved workflow: discretionary analysis, user execution, separate records from TPB-v1. These definitions make judgments reviewable; they have no established win rate or profitable expectancy. MPB-v1 and MBR-v1 were defined on 2026-09-28 and remain unchanged. FBR-v1 was approved for a separate paper comparison on 2026-10-02. All are spot longs; there is no obligation to trade each day.

Use closed candles only. For repeatable chart annotation, a confirmed swing high exceeds the highs of the two candles before and the two after it; a confirmed swing low is below the lows of those four candles. It is usable only after the second following candle closes. Equal extremes do not qualify under this convention. Record swing timestamps and prices, not hindsight labels. These are initial operational conventions, not optimized parameters.

## MPB-v1: trend pullback

1. Context: the last two confirmed 4-hour swing highs and last two confirmed swing lows are successively higher. Latest closed 4-hour candle remains above the most recent confirmed swing low. If the structure cannot be established, this setup does not qualify.
2. Location: before the trigger, mark a numeric support zone based on a previously broken confirmed 4-hour swing high. Record its boundaries, originating candles and most recent relevant resistance. A pullback must touch that zone; do not redraw it after seeing the outcome.
3. Trigger: after that touch, a completed 1-hour candle closes above the zone's upper boundary and above the previous hourly candle's high. Record the trigger candle and an explicit entry range/maximum derived from the stop, resistance and net reward/risk. No entry solely because a candle is green.
4. Invalidation: a completed hourly close below the zone's lower boundary before entry cancels the plan. Protective stop is below the lowest hourly low from first zone touch through trigger, by at least one price tick; specify any wider structural buffer before entry. Skip if the resulting risk/size or net reward/risk is unacceptable.
5. Target: a numeric target before the nearest identified overhead resistance, with its origin recorded. If that does not support at least 2:1 net reward/risk, reject the trade rather than push the target beyond resistance.

## MBR-v1: breakout retest

1. Context: same confirmed rising 4-hour swing structure as MPB-v1. This initial definition excludes countertrend breakouts.
2. Location: mark a numeric resistance zone around a confirmed 4-hour swing high before breakout; record boundary selection and candle timestamps. Identify the next overhead resistance separately.
3. Trigger: a completed 1-hour candle closes above the zone's upper boundary. A later hourly candle retests the marked zone and closes above its upper boundary. The retest must occur after the breakout candle; do not infer the sequence from one candle's high and low.
4. Invalidation: an hourly close below the lower boundary after breakout and before entry cancels the plan. Initial protective stop is below the retest candle's low by at least one tick; specify any wider structural buffer before entry. Record the trigger and numeric entry range; reject a price beyond the maximum.
5. Target: before the next identified overhead resistance. Reject if there is no defensible target providing at least 2:1 net reward/risk after costs.

## FBR-v1: faster breakout retest — paper only

Purpose: test whether an hourly resistance break and later hourly retest with 15-minute confirmation produces usable opportunities earlier than MPB-v1/MBR-v1. This is a new hypothesis, not a relaxation of their rules or a proven improvement. It can admit recovery trades that the older four-hour trend filter excludes. The timing, buffers and cost assumptions below are initial operational choices, not optimized parameters. No live use follows from a PAPER READY assessment.

“Faster” refers to possible eligibility during recovery, not a promise of a quick entry. From registration exactly on an hourly boundary, the earliest sequence takes 2 hours 15 minutes: one breakout hour, a separate retest hour, then 15m confirmation. Registration just after the boundary can make that nearly 3 hours 15 minutes, because the already-forming breakout hour is ineligible. Observation frequency does not shorten these candle requirements.

1. **Context and support.** Use only completed 4h candles. Record the last two confirmed 4h highs/lows with their confirmation times. Label the structure rising, falling or mixed; it is context, not a requirement that both highs and lows rise. Mark the most recent confirmed 4h swing low as support. The latest closed 4h close must remain strictly above it; missing support/context means DATA_MISSING. A later 4h close at or below the marked support cancels the watch. Freeze the support for that watch; do not lower it after a break.
2. **Premark the hourly level.** At observation time, select the most recent confirmed 1h swing high whose price is above the latest closed 1h close. Use the same two-candles-each-side definition at the 1h scale. Mark a zone one current exchange tick below/above that high and record the originating and confirmation timestamps. Mark the nearest confirmed 1h or 4h swing high strictly above the zone as overhead resistance, using the available snapshot history. Record any additional closer chart barrier and use it instead if relevant. If no defensible overhead resistance exists, leave the watch WAIT; do not invent a target. Keep all marked levels fixed. A new level is a new prospectively dated watch, never a retrospective revision.
3. **Breakout.** After registration, a completed 1h candle must close strictly above the zone's upper boundary. The breakout candle must start at or after registration: already-forming or historical breakouts cannot qualify for this forward trial. A watch expires six hours after registration if no entry occurs, or sooner at its event cutoff or invalidation.
4. **Retest and confirmation.** After the breakout hour closes, wait for the first later completed 1h candle whose range overlaps the zone (`low <= upper` and `high >= lower`). It must close strictly above the upper boundary. The retest hour must close within two hours of the breakout close. Then a separate completed 15m candle, starting at or after the retest hour closes, must close strictly above both the upper boundary and the immediately preceding 15m candle's high. Confirmation must close within one hour of the retest close. Any completed 1h close strictly below the lower boundary after breakout cancels the attempt; after the retest closes, any completed 15m close below the lower boundary also cancels it, even if price subsequently recovers. No inference of breakout, retest and confirmation from one candle. A first hourly retest that does not close above the zone ends this attempt; do not select a later successful retest retrospectively.
5. **Stop, target and entry.** Stop trigger is one price tick below the lower of the hourly retest low and all subsequent 15m lows through confirmation inclusive. Target is one price tick below the premarked nearest overhead resistance. Stop must be below entry and target above entry. Record both the maximum acceptable observed ask and the higher modeled fill price. Calculate these limits and quantity using the existing fee/inventory/rounding-aware risk calculator, with at least 2:1 net reward/risk. Do not tighten the stop or move the target to rescue a failing ratio. A forward paper entry needs an observed ask after confirmation, no older than 60 seconds, sufficient recorded book depth and a complete paper card. Its opportunity expires at the next 15m close after confirmation, or earlier on the watch deadline, event cutoff, support/zone invalidation, failed risk checks or price above the maximum. A breach of the planned stop before the observed paper entry cancels the confirmation; do not enter after a stop sweep and recovery. Entry attempts outside that window are MISSED_WINDOW, not backfilled trades.
6. **Costs and limits.** Use displayed account fees (currently 0.1% each side, subject to recheck), base-currency buy fee and quote-currency sell fee as the existing paper assumption, unless evidence requires another supported fee model. Let `w = (ask - bid) / ask` be the full observed spread fraction and `s = 0.0005` the base adverse slippage allowance (0.05%). These are declared modeling assumptions, not measured future execution. Require `w <= 0.001` (0.10%) and enough visible buy depth at or below the modeled entry fill for the planned quantity. For reproducible sizing set tools/risk.py `entry` to the observed ask, stop/target to their structural prices, and its uniform `slippage_rate` to `u = 1 - (1 - w/2) * (1 - s)`. Its tick-rounded entry is ask × (1 + u); stop/target fills are their reference × (1 - u). This deliberately applies an extra conservative allowance at entry even though the ask already includes spread; label it as such and add no further spread charge. Apply modeled fees once. If an exit uses an actually observed bid (including time exits), that bid already includes spread: apply s and the exit fee only. Fit the same default 0.5% risk, 1% portfolio cap, 2% daily/4% weekly halts, cash and instrument restrictions. Every quantity must be recomputed from its own shadow account, not assumed to be a 100-USDT position. Record all calculator inputs/outputs, observation times and instrument rounding.
7. **Management and events.** One full target, no partial exits, adding, stop widening or discretionary trailing. Scheduled time exit is four hours after the observed paper entry, shortened to the next event cutoff when applicable. Time exit uses the first available recorded bid at/after that deadline with costs; if monitoring was absent, mark late management and use the actual later observation, never a retrospectively assumed deadline price. For scheduled FOMC decisions, CPI, PCE and US employment reports, block entries from 30 minutes before until at least 30 minutes after release, and require spreads/depth to pass again afterward. Set the time exit to the earlier of entry plus four hours and 30 minutes before the next such release; reject an entry with less than 30 minutes available before that cutoff. Write exact UTC/Lagos times on the card. Unexpected event or data interruption blocks new entries and must be logged; it does not erase an open paper exposure.
8. **Paper outcome evidence.** Paper fills are modeled, never exchange fills. Use post-entry timestamped quotes or subsequent OHLC to assess exits, with explicit observation gaps. Stop/target tests begin only after the actual paper-entry timestamp; if it is inside a 15m candle, do not attribute that candle's earlier high/low to the position. Without finer data, a stop/target touch in that partial candle leaves the path unresolved; a later target cannot erase the possibility of an earlier stop. If neither threshold lies in that candle's full range, continue normally. Preserve unresolved path flags until finer evidence resolves them. Process evidence chronologically: the earliest established stop/target touch takes priority over any later observed quote or time exit. If stop and target are both touched in a fully post-entry candle and ordering is unknown, score stop first. If a bar opens below the stop, model the worse opening price before costs; a stop does not cap gap losses. Separate exposure from result certainty: possible remaining exposure stays open/marked and occupies the slot; if every feasible path is now flat under the declared full-fill model, release the slot and retain conservative cash/P&L bounds. For example, a possible earlier stop followed by a certain later exit means flat exposure with an uncertain result, not permission to score the later winner. Size from lower cash/equity bounds and apply conservative loss halts; scalar net P&L/R remains undefined until resolved by evidence. A full-fill model requires sufficient observed depth at entry and remains an assumption for exits. The protocol, observation helper and manual accounting helper are in validation.md and research.md; none places orders or opens paper positions automatically.

## Shared execution rules

- Record why each discretionary zone or closer resistance was selected, its source candles, and the alternatives considered before the trigger. MPB-v1/MBR-v1 zone widths and relevant barriers still involve judgment; do not describe them as a fully mechanical signal. FBR-v1's specified swing selection and one-tick zone remain fixed. Any new selection algorithm is a new version, not an undocumented tightening or relaxation.
- Before a setup becomes READY, fill every required trade-card field and confirm the account limits. Evidence may come from timestamped Bybit charts and quotes supplied by the user when the API is unavailable; clearly identify the source and any missing depth.
- The card must specify exact plan expiry, maximum holding time and time-exit action before entry. MPB-v1/MBR-v1 entry opportunity expires at the next 1-hour candle close following the trigger; FBR-v1 uses the next 15-minute close as specified above. Both expire sooner on invalidation, changed conditions or the stated deadline. Quote freshness still expires after 60 seconds: a candle-based opportunity is not permission to use an old quote.
- Record imminent macro events and an explicit event-exposure decision before entry. If the event's possible volatility cannot be accommodated by the execution/risk plan, wait. News alone never authorizes entry or relaxes the structural conditions.
- Single position; single fixed target by default. No adding to losses, widening stops or automatic breakeven/trailing changes. Any alternative management/partial-exit rule must be written before entry, sized correctly and evaluated separately.
- Plan the stop mechanism, net sellable inventory and what the user will do if protection fails. If no workable protection/exit contingency can be established, keep the plan CONDITIONAL or WAIT. Do not count an unsubmitted or unconfirmed order as active protection.
- Changing swing conventions, trigger rules or management after observing results requires a new version and a recorded rationale. Preserve original cards and results. These setups are separate from TPB-v1 and its frozen research periods.
